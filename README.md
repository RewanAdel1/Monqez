# Monqez
# 📎 Project Name: Monqez — Your Trusted Companion on Every Road

## 📌 Project Overview

Monqez is a travel assistance platform concept designed to make road trips in Egypt safer, easier, and more predictable.

The platform helps travelers discover essential services along their routes, including fuel stations, restaurants, rest stops, towing services, vehicle repair workshops, and medical facilities.

Monqez focuses on route-based service discovery, verified providers, transparent pricing, and authentic customer reviews. Its goal is to help travelers make informed decisions, avoid unexpected costs, and access reliable assistance during their journeys.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Simplify the discovery of essential services along travel routes.
* Help users compare service providers by distance, ratings, and availability.
* Promote transparent pricing and reduce unexpected service costs.
* Improve user trust through provider verification and authentic reviews.
* Design an intuitive experience for roadside assistance requests.
* Develop a consistent user experience for travelers and service providers.
* Validate the design through usability testing and feedback.

## 📦 Project Scope

### Traveler Experience

* Onboarding and location selection.
* Route planning and map-based service discovery.
* Service categories, search, and filters.
* Distance-based listings and provider profiles.
* Provider verification indicators and customer reviews.
* Transparent pricing and booking confirmation.
* Roadside assistance requests and status tracking.
* Post-service ratings and feedback.

### Service Provider Experience

* Provider registration and profile management.
* Service listings and pricing management.
* Availability and request management.

### Admin Dashboard Concept

* Provider verification and approval.
* Service listing management.
* Review and complaint monitoring.

### UX/UI Deliverables

* User research and competitor analysis.
* User personas and journey maps.
* Information architecture and user flows.
* Low-fidelity wireframes.
* High-fidelity UI designs.
* Design system and reusable components.
* Interactive Figma prototype.
* Usability testing and design iterations.
